General Knowledge True or False Quiz

25 GK statements from many subjects. Decide whether each one is true or false, then tap it to check the answer.

General knowledge questions are often about facts that most people think they know: who invented what, which planet is largest, what a word really means. Easy questions are mixed here with commonly confused ones, so a quick quiz also shows the facts you have been getting wrong.

True or false is the simplest quiz format. It works for a quick daily review, for warming up before the general awareness part of an exam, or just for fun.

25 GK questions

These are 25 of the 300 questions in the app’s example deck, spread across all topics.

  1. Thomas Edison invented the telephone.

    X · False The telephone is credited to Alexander Graham Bell.

  2. "Quickly" is an adverb.

    O · True

  3. GPS works using signals sent through undersea cables.

    X · False GPS uses signals from satellites.

  4. The Summer Olympic Games are held every four years.

    O · True

  5. Eating lots of carrots lets you see in the dark.

    X · False Carrots help keep eyes healthy but do not give night vision.

  6. World War I began in 1914.

    O · True

  7. A synonym is a word with the opposite meaning.

    X · False A synonym has a similar meaning; an antonym has the opposite meaning.

  8. The skin makes vitamin D when exposed to sunlight.

    O · True

  9. Tacos are a traditional dish from Italy.

    X · False Tacos come from Mexico.

  10. "Level" is a palindrome.

    O · True

  11. Arabic is written from right to left.

    O · True

  12. Most of the Amazon rainforest is in Argentina.

    X · False Most of the Amazon rainforest is in Brazil.

  13. The equator passes through Europe.

    X · False The equator crosses South America, Africa and Asia, but not Europe.

  14. An octopus has eight arms.

    O · True

  15. Ocean tides are caused mainly by the wind.

    X · False Tides are caused mainly by the Moon's gravity.

  16. A haiku is a short form of Japanese poetry.

    O · True

  17. Greenland is a continent.

    X · False Greenland is an island, not a continent.

  18. Earth spins once on its axis roughly every 24 hours.

    O · True

  19. In traditional color theory, the primary colors are green, orange and purple.

    X · False The traditional primary colors are red, yellow and blue.

  20. The Sun is a star.

    O · True

  21. In boxing, hitting below the belt is allowed.

    X · False Hitting below the belt is against the rules.

  22. Nelson Mandela was the first president of the United States.

    X · False George Washington was the first U.S. president; Mandela was president of South Africa.

  23. GPS uses signals from satellites.

    O · True

  24. Australia lies in the Northern Hemisphere.

    X · False Australia lies in the Southern Hemisphere.

  25. Vaccines help the immune system recognize germs.

    O · True

Make it stick

If you miss one, read the answer and say the corrected fact once out loud. Coming back to the same questions a few days later is what moves them into long-term memory.
