Science True or False Quiz

25 general science statements, from physics and chemistry to biology and Earth science. Decide first, then tap a statement to check.

Many science facts are easy to get backwards: which layer of the Earth is the largest, whether heavier objects fall faster, what plants take in and give off. These statements mix well-established facts with common misconceptions, so they are good for checking real understanding, not just recognition.

Topics cover force and motion, electricity and magnetism, light and sound, matter, chemical reactions, cells and genetics, the human body, plants, animals and ecology, and Earth and space.

25 science questions

These are 25 of the 300 questions in the app’s example deck, spread across all topics.

  1. The largest layer of Earth's interior by volume is the crust.

    X · False The largest layer by volume is the mantle.

  2. The bright stars Vega and Altair can be seen in the summer night sky of the Northern Hemisphere.

    O · True

  3. The large intestine is where most nutrients in food are absorbed.

    X · False Nutrients are absorbed mainly in the small intestine; the large intestine mainly absorbs water.

  4. Speed is the distance traveled divided by the time taken.

    O · True

  5. White light, such as sunlight, is light of a single color.

    X · False White light (sunlight) is a mixture of light of many colors.

  6. The kidneys filter waste from the blood and make urine.

    O · True

  7. The nucleus is the main place where a cell makes energy.

    X · False Energy is made mainly in the mitochondria. The nucleus holds the genetic material.

  8. An octopus has 10 arms.

    X · False An octopus has 8 arms.

  9. Sunsets are red because much of the blue light is scattered as sunlight passes through a thick layer of atmosphere.

    O · True

  10. Mixing all three primary colors of light gives black.

    X · False Mixing all three primary colors of light gives white.

  11. Sex cells such as sperm and eggs have twice as many chromosomes as body cells.

    X · False Sex cells have half as many chromosomes as body cells.

  12. Electrolysis of water produces hydrogen and oxygen.

    O · True

  13. Most mules, born from a horse and a donkey, cannot have offspring.

    O · True

  14. Salt water is a pure substance.

    X · False Salt water is a mixture of salt and water.

  15. Cumulonimbus clouds bring showers, thunder and lightning.

    O · True

  16. Scientific names are, as a rule, written in English.

    X · False Scientific names are written in Latin (or Latinized words).

  17. Carbon monoxide has no color or smell, so it is hard to notice.

    O · True

  18. Groundwater exists only as large underground rivers or lakes.

    X · False Most groundwater soaks into the gaps between soil and rocks.

  19. Sweat lowers body temperature by taking heat from the body as it evaporates.

    O · True

  20. Rubber and glass are good conductors of electricity.

    X · False Rubber and glass are insulators (nonconductors) that do not conduct electricity well.

  21. Lithium-ion batteries are commonly used in mobile phones.

    O · True

  22. Plant leaves look green because they absorb green light.

    X · False Leaves look green because they reflect green light.

  23. Stars seem to twinkle because of the movement of Earth's atmosphere.

    O · True

  24. Heat moves on its own from a cold object to a hot object.

    X · False On its own, heat moves only from hot places to cold places.

  25. A place where bones are connected so that they can move is called a joint.

    O · True

Make it stick

If you miss one, read the answer and think about why the false version sounds believable. Misconceptions are hard to unlearn, and noticing them is half the work.
