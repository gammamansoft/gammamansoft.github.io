XXL Contacts : Big Font

Your phone contacts in extra-large text. Tap a name to call or text. No ads.

Get it on Google Play (free)

Is small text on your phone making it hard to call people?

XXL Contacts is a contacts app for people with low vision or farsightedness and for older adults. It shows your phone contacts in extra-large text, and you can call or send a message with a tap.

Extra-large text

  • Contact names fill the screen in large, easy-to-read text.
  • 8 text sizes. Change one step at a time with "Larger Text" and "Smaller Text" in the menu (☰).
  • Uses the size you choose in this app, regardless of your phone's font size setting.
  • Your text size and view are kept the next time you open the app.

Tap to call or text

  • Tap a name to show large CALL and MESSAGE buttons.
  • CALL opens your phone app with the number already entered. You tap the call button once more to place the call, which helps prevent calls by accident.

4 views

  • Name only / Name + number / Photo + name / Photo + name + number
  • Contact photos are shown in circles.

Easy to find

  • Starred family and friends come first, then everyone else by name.
  • Tap the magnifier to search by part of a name or number.

Easy from the start

  • Contacts are imported right away when you allow access on first launch.
  • "App Help" in the menu explains everything in large text. You can make the help text even larger with A− / A+.

10 languages

  • English, Korean, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Spanish, German, French, Russian, Hindi and Arabic.
  • The app follows your phone's language, and you can choose another language for this app only in the menu: "Language".

Safe to use

  • Only reads your phone contacts. Never adds, changes or deletes them.
  • No internet permission, so your contacts are never sent anywhere.
  • No ads.
  • Only one permission: Contacts.

Good to know

  • After adding or changing contacts on your phone, tap "Import Phone Contacts" in the menu.
